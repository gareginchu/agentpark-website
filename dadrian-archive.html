Digital Archive

The world's most comprehensive digital repository of primary sources, testimonies, and scholarship on the Armenian Genocide — assembled over five decades by Professor Vahakn N. Dadrian.

Declassified state archives, Ottoman court-martial records, diplomatic dispatches, survivor testimonies, and Prof. Dadrian's annotated research — fully indexed and searchable.

AI-Native Architecture

The first AI-native digital archive in the world of Genocide and Holocaust scholarship. Every document is processed by AI — auto-titled, described, and entity-tagged — and embedded in semantic vector space. All 660 GB of the archive is fully searchable by meaning, not just keywords, across Armenian and Latin scripts.
