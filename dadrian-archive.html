Prof. Vahakn N. Dadrian Digital Archive

The world's most comprehensive digital repository of primary sources, testimonies, and scholarship on the Armenian Genocide — assembled over five decades by Prof. Vahakn N. Dadrian.

Official Public Launch

The archive opens to researchers, educators, and the public on November 15, 2026. Until then, access is by invitation only for selected reviewers.

The Collection

Declassified state archives, Ottoman court-martial records, diplomatic dispatches, survivor testimonies, and Prof. Dadrian's annotated research — fully indexed and searchable.
